About Hilltop Market

Since 1985, Hilltop Market has been a cornerstone of the Chula Vista community. What started as a small family-owned butcher shop has grown into a full-service market, but we've never forgotten our roots.

We believe in old-fashioned values: quality products, fair prices, and genuine personal service. Our team knows many of our customers by name, and we treat everyone who walks through our doors like family.

Whether you're picking up tonight's dinner, stocking your pantry, or planning a special meal, we're here to help. Stop by and experience the Hilltop Market difference for yourself.
